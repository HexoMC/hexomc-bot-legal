1. Who operates the bot

HexoMC operates the official HexoMC Discord bot. This policy covers information handled to provide the bot’s functions. It does not cover independently hosted copies, Discord itself, or unrelated Minecraft services. Contact the HexoMC bot operator through the staff of the Discord server where you use the bot. If you cannot access that server, open a contact request and ask for a private communication channel. Do not post personal information or message contents in a public issue.

02

2. Information processed

Depending on enabled features and permissions, the bot processes:
  • Discord identifiers and profile information: user, server, channel, role and message IDs; usernames, display names, avatars, server membership and roles; account creation and join information.
  • Messages and interactions: accessible message text, command arguments, mentions, reactions, button interactions, and attachment metadata or URLs, for command handling, filtering and logging.
  • Moderation: warnings, infractions, affected user and moderator IDs, reasons, automatic moderation incident details, actions, durations and timestamps.
  • Activity and community features: XP, levels, message activity timestamps, voice-channel join information and session duration, temporary voice-channel ownership, giveaway details and participant IDs.
  • Server configuration: permissions, role mappings, filter rules, exempt users, log destinations and excluded channels.
  • External alerts: administrator-supplied YouTube channel IDs or TikTok usernames, destination channels and latest observed public upload IDs.
  • Operational information: errors and event metadata needed to operate and troubleshoot the bot.

03

3. Message content and deleted messages

The bot processes accessible messages for moderation and other enabled features. When message logging is configured, available deleted message content, attachment filenames or image URLs, and before-and-after edited text may be copied into a designated Discord log channel. Deleting the original message does not automatically delete that log entry. Access to those logs depends on the server’s channel permissions. Attachment previews may stop working when Discord removes the original file.

04

4. Voice activity

Voice-state events and timing are used for XP and temporary voice-channel management. These features do not require recording conversations; the reviewed implementation does not record voice audio.

05

5. Why information is used

Information is used to execute commands, manage roles and channels, operate community features, detect spam and rule violations, maintain moderation records, display ranks and giveaway results, deliver configured alerts, and diagnose service errors. Data should not be submitted to the bot for unrelated purposes.

06

6. Storage, visibility and service providers

Persistent bot records are stored in the operator’s database. Some recent event and message information is held in memory. Logs, command responses, rankings and giveaway results may also be posted to Discord, where users with access to the destination can see them. The operator and authorized personnel may access data as necessary to operate the service and handle requests. Discord and infrastructure providers process information needed to deliver their services. Information may be disclosed where legally required. The bot’s described functions do not sell personal data or provide advertising profiles.

07

7. External services

Configured YouTube and TikTok alerts request public content for the selected channel or username. Those services receive the requested public identifier and ordinary server request information, such as the bot host’s IP address; these requests do not require your Discord password. Following an external link is governed by that service’s own policies. Discord’s handling of data is explained in its Privacy Policy.

08

8. Retention and deletion

Different records serve different purposes: configuration supports active features; moderation history supports moderation; XP and giveaway records support those features. The current implementation does not establish one automatic expiry period for all stored records. Leaving a server or removing the bot does not automatically erase all existing data. HexoMC must delete API data promptly when it is no longer needed for the bot’s permitted functionality, when the service ends, or upon a valid user or Discord deletion request, except where retention is required by law. Discord log messages and any operational backups must be considered separately when fulfilling a request; deletion of a database row alone does not remove those copies.

09

9. Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your bot data by contacting the operator using the contact route above. Identify the relevant Discord user ID and server privately, and describe your request. We may ask for reasonable verification of account ownership, but never your password or account token. Additional rights may apply under local law. Removing data can reset ranks or affect feature availability. Ask server administrators about messages they control; the operator will address bot-controlled copies and coordinate where needed. You may also stop using optional interactions, and administrators may disable features or remove the bot.

10

10. Security and children

Access to bot data should be limited to people who need it to operate the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords, financial information, or other sensitive information to the bot. The bot is not intended for anyone below Discord’s minimum permitted age. Contact the operator if you believe an underage person’s data has been collected.

11. This website and policy updates

These policy pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, or application cookies. The website host may process normal request logs, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. For GitHub Pages, see GitHub’s privacy statement. Changes to this policy will be published here with a revised date, with appropriate notice for material changes.
