You must be at least 13 years old and meet the minimum age required in your country and by Discord. You must comply with Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, applicable laws, and the rules of the server in which you use the bot.

3. What the bot provides

Depending on configuration and permissions, the bot provides moderation, automated spam and content filtering, event logging, automatic and selectable roles, XP and leaderboards, giveaways, temporary voice channels, and notifications of public YouTube and TikTok uploads. Features may change or become unavailable.

4. Administrator responsibilities

Review the permissions you grant, restrict access to moderation commands and log channels, and tell members which logging and automated features are enabled. Administrators remain responsible for their rules, moderation decisions, and giveaway terms and prizes. Do not submit secrets, login credentials, or sensitive personal information in commands or moderation reasons.
